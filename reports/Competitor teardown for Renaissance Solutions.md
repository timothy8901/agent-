## Context: what RS sells, to whom, and why this teardown (drawn from RS's Linear records)

*Everything in this section comes from RS's own Linear workspace and Drive glossary exports, read 2026-09-27 ([Linear] tag). None of it is independent evidence of demand.*

- **Your company's product/service.**
  - **Working description:** "We help organizations explore what's possible and build what matters." Front-door promise: "You don't need to know what service you need before you talk to us"; RS will sometimes tell a client "**not to spend the money**" ([REN-462](https://linear.app/renaissance-solutions/issue/REN-462/create-who-we-are-skill-for-context)).
  - **Mission line in Mike's article draft:** "help small businesses and organizations understand, adopt, and use rapidly changing technology — especially AI"; "Controls scale with size; accountability does not disappear" ([Draft v2, "The AI Did It" Is Not an Excuse](https://linear.app/renaissance-solutions/document/draft-v2-the-ai-did-it-is-not-an-excuse-2432c56f0e4c)).
  - **Offer structure (approved by Mike 2026-09-25):** three ways to start: (1) "Use it yourself — free" (assessment forms, no contact details required, results not RS-reviewed); (2) "Report and follow-up — paid" (one defined question, recommendations report, one-hour call); (3) "Assessment, design and plan — paid" (implementation optional and separately scoped). The approved question menu covers one workflow, recovery for one service, an AI product adoption or renewal, a team's existing AI use, learning/workshop support, communication for one audience, and "Other". **Prices are still `[PRICE]` placeholders; the scope model is unselected; no checkout exists** ([REN-1068](https://linear.app/renaissance-solutions/issue/REN-1068/expand-working-with-us-service-philosophy-assessment-options-and)).
  - **Governance menu:** 24 services and 33 artifacts across three packages plus a retainer track. On 2026-09-27: 1 service Done (Vendor AI due diligence), 1 in human verification (Shadow AI discovery), 5 Todo, 17 Backlog; no artifact is Done ([REN-469](https://linear.app/renaissance-solutions/issue/REN-469/governance-service-menu-build-out); [Value Proposition project](https://linear.app/renaissance-solutions/project/value-proposition-cf219e221760)).
  - **What makes it distinct (per RS's own scans):** education-first, plain-language "AI Literacy for the Rest of Us" ([RS Learning Lab glossary page](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1A45DEjSsqwwc2bbmm9aZYNawWU5v2bi-/view?usp=drivesdk)); free no-contact self-serve forms built on the Bespoke Forms engine ([REN-1062](https://linear.app/renaissance-solutions/issue/REN-1062/where-do-i-start-assessment-navigator-with-visible-landing-page)); "Clients choose who implements and when; no continuing engagement is required" ([REN-1068](https://linear.app/renaissance-solutions/issue/REN-1068/expand-working-with-us-service-philosophy-assessment-options-and)); a Curious Boomer older-adult brand, whose glossary shorts are RS's one clearly live public channel ([REN-5](https://linear.app/renaissance-solutions/issue/REN-5/shadow-ai)).
- **Target market/niche (ICP).**
  - The competitor board tracks firms "targeting small to mid-size organizations" ([P-REN-18](https://linear.app/renaissance-solutions/project/competitor-research-18e465cf0c71)).
  - Practice persona **"Priya (Accidental AI Champion)"**: an operations or program manager at a **120-person nonprofit, municipality or professional organization**; "Budget is constrained and politically visible. Procurement exists"; she can champion but "usually cannot alone buy or implement". Her jobs: discover what employees already do, find use cases, set boundaries, educate staff "without frightening them", give leadership a roadmap, and "avoid becoming permanent AI technical support" ([REN-591](https://linear.app/renaissance-solutions/issue/REN-591/practice-with-priya-accidental-ai-champion-jim)).

  - Geography: Chicago's northwest suburbs (Mount Prospect, Arlington Heights, Des Plaines; "NW suburbs / Cook-DuPage-Lake") ([P-REN-4](https://linear.app/renaissance-solutions/project/village-letters-03b23c492acc); [REN-1090](https://linear.app/renaissance-solutions/issue/REN-1090/chicagoland-peer-scan-2026-09-25)).
