### Inferences
- In practice, "what RS sells today" is a free tier plus a paid tier that is designed but not priced. The paid offers closest to ready are "report and follow-up" questions that reuse the two advanced forms:
  - "resolve questions before adopting/renewing an AI product" → Vendor AI due diligence;
  - "prioritize action on a team's existing AI use" → Shadow AI discovery.
- Positioning tension:
  - REN-462 positions RS broadly ("explore what's possible and build what matters").
  - REN-469 and REN-473 describe a narrow, committee-heavy governance menu.
  - The question-based "Ways to start" menu (REN-1068) bridges the two.
  - Positioning statements should probably lead with plain-language questions and treat governance artifacts as deliverables, not as the headline.
- Charters, committees, standing chairs and quarterly reporting look heavy for a Priya-type 120-person organization, and heavier still for a 5–50-person firm. RS's own "controls scale with size" line argues for leading with three things: Package 1 diagnostics, the plain-language Acceptable Use Policy, and literacy training.
- Package naming is inconsistent. REN-473 uses "Package A/B/C" with product names; REN-493–496 use "Package 1/2/3" by category. This needs reconciling before any public price list.
