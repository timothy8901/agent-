## 1. Company analysis: core business, problem solved, customer base, marketing and sales wins, SWOT

### Takeaway
All three sell vendor-independent, part-time senior technology leadership to organizations too small for a full-time CIO, but they are built differently:
- **Fluent Consultants** (Kansas City area; founded 1999 as Trabon Solutions) pairs fractional-CIO advice with its own software, data and AI build teams.
- **Hartman Executive Advisors** (Timonium, MD; 2004) is a team of about 80–85 advisors, apparently employees, on monthly retainers, with a complete AI governance, strategy and training line.
- **Freeman Clarke** (Oxford, UK; 2012) is a bench of 100+ board-level "Principals" claiming 1,300+ clients, expanding into the US and Canada under a US CEO hired in 2023.

Hartman is the closest match to RS's AI governance menu.
