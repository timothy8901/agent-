## 1. Company analysis: core business, problem solved, customer base, marketing and sales wins, SWOT

### Takeaway
TeamLogic IT is a franchise MSP with 350+ locations and network revenue above $235M in 2025. It now sells "Managed AI" (readiness assessments, data governance, monitoring, consulting) and publishes AI-leadership content for SMB executives, so its entry offer overlaps RS's flagship. CMIT Solutions is a 30+-year franchise MSP based in Austin. It positions itself "security-first," with managed detection and response (MDR), compliance (including CMMC) and Managed AI. Both sell outsourced or co-managed IT to SMBs through locally owned offices, and neither publishes prices.

## 2. Financial and operational: funding and ownership, revenue and growth, headcount and recent hires, organization model

### Takeaway
TeamLogic is a privately held franchisor. It has historically been 100% owned by Franchise Services, Inc. (FSI) of Mission Viejo, CA, and has had no outside funding rounds. Its big 2026 structural change: in May 2026 it bought its largest franchisee, IT Assist (backed by the private-equity firms Salt Creek Capital and Plexus Capital). That made it a hybrid operator with 30 company-owned locations in five states, one of them Illinois. Network revenue topped $235M in 2025, and the 2025 FDD shows a median of about $679K revenue for units open 24+ months. CMIT's ownership, unit count and financials could not be verified in this pass.

### Inferences
- **Franchisor revenue: Not public. Estimate: TeamLogic, LLC's 2026 run-rate revenue is roughly $35–50M [Estimate].**
  - Royalties ≈ 7% × $235M ≈ $16M. This is an upper bound; whether hardware pass-through counts toward royalties is unknown.
  - Marketing fund ≈ 2% × $235M ≈ $4.7M, restricted use.
  - Initial fees ≈ 41 openings × $40K–$49.5K ≈ $1.6–2.0M.
  - Company-owned revenue from May 2026 ≈ 30 locations × $0.7–1.0M ≈ $21–30M annualized.
- **Average revenue per location ≈ $0.7M [Estimate]** ($235M ÷ roughly 310–350 locations). This matches the 2025 FDD median of about $679K. The FDD average of more than $1M is pulled up by large multi-territory operators.
- **"Locations" and "franchisees" are different counts.** The network has 350+ locations, yet the 24-month cohort has 161 franchisees and one aggregator shows 282 "active units." The gap exists because multi-territory owners hold many locations; IT Assist alone had 23 territories.
- **Growth trend [Estimate]:**
  - 14% comp growth plus roughly 13% unit growth (41 openings on a base near 310) implies network revenue growth in the mid-teens or faster for 2025.
  - The 500-by-2030 target implies roughly 7–8% annual unit growth from 350.
- **Network headcount: Not public. Estimate: about 2,400–3,000 [Estimate].** That is 1,700+ technicians (about 5 per location) plus an owner and 1–2 sales or admin staff per location. The franchisor's support-center headcount is not public.
- **What the IT Assist buy changes:** equity held through PE-backed franchisee owners (Salt Creek, Plexus) moves onto the franchisor's own balance sheet. The franchisor is now also an operator competing for SMB clients in five states, including Illinois.
